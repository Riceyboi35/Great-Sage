# Ideal customer (TradieFlow AI)

## Who
- Small Australian tradies and service businesses, usually owner-operators or
  small crews.
- **First group to trial with:** cabinet makers, joiners and 2pac sprayers — the
  trade Dan works in and knows people in.
- **Next:** plumbers, electricians, landscapers, cleaners, painters, builders,
  mechanics, concreters.

## Problems TradieFlow solves (from the planned features)
- Missing calls while on the tools, and losing the job.
- Leads and quotes scattered across texts, notes and memory.
- Quotes that never get followed up.
- Forgetting supplies for upcoming jobs.
- Chasing payment.
